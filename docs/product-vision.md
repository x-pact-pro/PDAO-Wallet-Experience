# PrivateDAO Wallet Atelier — Product Vision

**Status:** In progress

## Audience

People who manage meaningful digital assets, move between chains, and need clear control over every signature. They value quiet confidence, exact numbers, and fast access to market context.

## Promise

A private-by-design, self-custody financial workspace: a composed home for assets, a live intelligence desk for markets, and a precise signing surface for payments and trades.

## Signature experience

A calm, interactive welcome: a crafted PDAO object turns slowly in a field of fine orbital lines. One clear action opens a useful portfolio. Market energy belongs in the market pages, where motion follows real price updates and never implies a forecast.

## Product principles

- Local custody: secret material is created, encrypted, decrypted, and signed on the user's device.
- Exact before action: show chain, asset, amount, recipient, fees, expiry, and visibility before signing.
- Real data: live prices and service catalog come from their providers; unavailable data stays explicitly unavailable.
- One workspace across web, Chrome, and Android, adapted to each platform's navigation and privacy model.
- Paid service prices come from the Agent Exchange catalog. Any PDAO utility conversion follows the live quote and fee contract, never a guessed rate or recipient.
- Calm by default; vivid market signals on demand. Reduced-motion preferences are honored.
