PrivateDAO Wallet brings on-chain assets and financial activity into one considered workspace.

• Create or restore a wallet. Recovery phrases are encrypted locally with your passphrase.
• Connect compatible wallets available in your browser or Android wallet environment.
• View supported network balances, market prices, and price movements.
• Review and authorize transactions with your wallet before they are submitted.
• Explore PrivatePAY and Agent Exchange services where available.

Recovery phrases and private keys are not sent to PDAO services. Live balances, prices, and remote services require network access and depend on supported networks and providers.
