PrivateDAO Wallet brings your digital-asset workspace into a calm, carefully designed experience.

Explore live market prices, discover movement across tracked assets, and browse the PrivateDAO Agent Exchange catalog. Create or restore a recovery phrase on your device and encrypt it locally with a passphrase.

PrivatePAY can request the current payment requirements from Agent Exchange and show the provider response for review. The app does not claim a payment was sent unless a future release implements and confirms the complete wallet-authorized settlement flow.

The Android application is built from the public source with Capacitor. Market and service data are fetched from their named providers. Wallet recovery phrases are not sent to those providers.
