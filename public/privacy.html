Privacy policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026 · Draft for product launch

This policy explains what information the new PrivateDAO Wallet application handles when you use its screens and connected services.

Your recovery phrase and private keys are designed to remain on your device. Never enter them into a support chat, identity provider, or third-party service.

1. Wallet information

Public wallet addresses and signed transactions are visible on public blockchain networks. The application may use a wallet address to request balances, quotes, or transaction data from the network and selected service providers. A public address is not a private key, but it can be associated with public activity.

2. Local data and keys

Wallet creation and key management are intended to happen locally in your browser or Android app. Encrypted wallet data, if you choose to save it, stays in local application storage. Recovery phrases and private keys are not sent to Auth0, Descope, Agent Exchange, market-data providers, or PrivateDAO APIs. Clearing app data or losing the device may remove locally stored wallet information.

3. Identity services

Auth0 may handle user sign-in and account identity when you choose to sign in. Descope may handle agent identity, authentication, or authorization for agent-related features when those capabilities are enabled. These providers receive the information necessary to perform their respective identity functions under their privacy policies. Identity credentials are separate from wallet signing keys.

4. Connected services and network requests

When you open market, Agent Exchange, portfolio, or PrivatePAY features, your device communicates with the relevant provider. Providers may receive your IP address, request metadata, and any public wallet address or transaction details needed to respond. Portfolio balances are read directly from Solana and Ethereum mainnet RPC providers, including Solana Labs, Cloudflare, and PublicNode, with fallback when a provider is unavailable. Market prices are requested from CoinGecko. Agent capabilities and payment operations are requested from the PrivateDAO Agent Exchange. Requests may be visible to the relevant blockchain network when submitted.

5. Storage, analytics, and sharing

The current application build does not intentionally add advertising trackers or analytics. We do not sell recovery phrases or private keys. If optional analytics, cloud backup, or account synchronization is added later, the application should identify that feature and obtain any required consent before collecting or syncing additional data.

6. Security

We use a self-custody design, local signing, and encrypted local storage where implemented. No software can guarantee complete security. Keep your device and browser updated, use a strong unlock passphrase, and maintain a private offline backup of your recovery phrase.

7. Your choices and contact

You can stop using connected services and remove local application data from your device. Identity-provider accounts can be managed through those providers. For privacy questions, use the support channel published on privatedao.org.
